# Client Onboarding and Delivery for Social Media Services

A professional client experience begins after the customer agrees to work with you. Clear onboarding prevents misunderstandings and protects both sides.

## 1. Confirm the agreement

Record the agreed service, deliverables, platforms, timeline, price, payment terms, revision limits and cancellation/refund terms.

## 2. Collect only what you need

Ask for brand assets, content references, business information and approved access through official platform permission tools where possible.

Never ask a client to send passwords or one-time verification codes through WhatsApp, DMs, text or email.

## 3. Create a simple project record

Track:

- Client name and contact preference.
- Agreed scope.
- Payment status.
- Required assets and approvals.
- Milestones and due dates.
- Work completed.
- Open questions and next actions.

## 4. Communicate progress

Agree how often the client will receive updates. Tell them early if a dependency, approval or external platform issue affects the timeline.

## 5. Review and close

At the end of the project:

- Confirm which deliverables were completed.
- Share agreed reports or files.
- Record any remaining tasks.
- Ask for feedback.
- Request permission before using work publicly as a case study.

## 6. Handle problems professionally

Acknowledge the issue, check the facts, explain available options and follow the written agreement. Do not promise an outcome that depends on a third-party platform or provider.
