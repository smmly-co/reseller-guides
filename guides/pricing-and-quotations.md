# Pricing, Quotations and Payments for SMM Resellers

Pricing should cover delivery costs, operating effort and the risk of supporting a customer—not just produce a markup over a provider rate.

## 1. Calculate your real cost

Include:

- Direct service or provider cost.
- Payment fees.
- Currency conversion.
- Labour and support time.
- Software and operating expenses.
- Taxes and compliance costs.
- A reasonable allowance for disputes or eligible refunds.

## 2. Choose a pricing model

Common approaches include:

- Fixed project fee.
- Monthly retainer.
- Per-deliverable pricing.
- Hourly consulting or production.
- Catalogue service pricing with a clearly defined quantity and unit.

Choose a model the customer can understand and you can deliver profitably.

## 3. Write a quotation

Every quotation should specify:

- Service description.
- Deliverables and quantities.
- Timeline and dependencies.
- Total price and payment schedule.
- Taxes, where applicable.
- Revision and change-request terms.
- Cancellation and refund policy.
- Expiry date and acceptance method.

## 4. Example quotation structure

**Service:** Monthly short-form content support  
**Deliverables:** 8 edited videos and 8 captions  
**Platforms:** Instagram and YouTube Shorts  
**Timeline:** Four weeks after receiving approved assets  
**Fee:** [Your quoted amount]  
**Payment:** [Agreed schedule]  
**Exclusions:** Filming, advertising spend and additional revisions  
**Approval:** Written confirmation by the client

## 5. Avoid misleading pricing

Do not advertise an unusually low entry price while hiding required add-ons or conditions. Explain recurring charges, minimum quantities and what happens when a service cannot be completed as expected.
