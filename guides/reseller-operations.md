# Running SMM Reseller Operations

A reseller business needs repeatable processes for sales, orders, money and customer support.

## Daily operating routine

1. Review new enquiries.
2. Confirm which requests fit your service scope.
3. Send quotations and record approvals.
4. Check payment status.
5. Submit and monitor approved work.
6. Update customers when meaningful progress occurs.
7. Resolve support requests and record outcomes.
8. Reconcile payments, provider costs and customer balances.

## Separate sales from fulfilment

Do not treat a customer's payment as proof that an order has been successfully delivered. Track payment, order submission and fulfilment as separate states.

## Set customer support expectations

Publish working hours, normal response windows and the information customers should include when contacting you. Never ask customers for passwords or OTPs.

## Use automation carefully

Automation can help organise enquiries, reminders and order records. It should not send unsolicited bulk messages, make unsupported promises or submit duplicate orders after uncertain responses.

## Know when to use software

A spreadsheet may be enough for a small number of projects. A CRM, order tracker or SMM panel becomes useful when manual records create errors or slow down service. Choose tools based on actual workload.
