# SMM Services and Order Management

Order management is more than submitting a request. A reseller needs to understand the service, verify the order details and communicate accurately.

## Before placing an order

Confirm:

- Correct service and platform.
- Correct customer destination or URL.
- Quantity is within the stated limits.
- The destination meets service requirements.
- The customer understands the service description and limitations.
- Price and terms have been approved.

## Keep order records

Maintain the customer reference, provider order reference, service, quantity, submitted time, amount charged and current status. Restrict access to this information.

## Monitor status changes

Use the provider's documented status definitions. If the status has not changed, check the provider's guidance before submitting another order.

## Partial, refill, cancellation and refund requests

Eligibility varies by service. Check the applicable terms and order status before making a request. Explain the decision and any adjustment to the customer in plain language.

## Do not overpromise

A status update is not proof of sales, leads, audience quality or long-term engagement. Explain what the service does and what it does not guarantee.
